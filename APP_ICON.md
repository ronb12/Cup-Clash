# App Icon

The `AppIcon` asset catalog is in place. No generated placeholder PNGs were added.

## Required asset

Add a single **1024 × 1024** PNG to:

`CupClash/Resources/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/`

and reference it from `Contents.json` as `"filename" : "AppIcon.png"`.

## Concept

- One white arcade ball flying toward a triangular rack of cups
- Near-black navy background
- Electric cyan and bright violet neon lighting
- Strong depth
- No words, alcohol imagery, or copyrighted graphics
