Cup Clash: Pong Rivals is a family-friendly 3D arcade cup-toss for iPhone. Aim, throw, and clear the other side’s cups in fast, colorful matches.

Play your way:
• Quick Match against Rookie, Pro, or Champion AI
• Pass & Play on one device
• Practice with unlimited throws
• Daily Challenge with a new seeded match every day
• Weekly tournament: Opening, Semifinal, and Final
• Special challenges with unique objectives
• Optional Sudden Death — first to 3 cups

Career and social:
• Unlock balls, cups, and arenas in the Locker
• Local and Game Center leaderboards
• Achievements and friend ranks
• Invite Game Center friends and compare weekly cup scores

Clear, readable throwing: drag on the table to aim, pull back for power, and tap Throw. The camera swings around the table each turn.

Family-friendly: cups hold water, soda, or colorful fictional drinks. There is no alcohol, gambling, or intoxication.
